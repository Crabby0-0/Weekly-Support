A few quick answers

Can I plan without an internet connection?

Yes. You can use your planner offline, and changes are saved on your device. If you are signed in, cloud syncing needs an internet connection. Signing in also requires a connection.

Why am I not receiving reminders?

Check that reminders are enabled in Weekly and that notifications are allowed in your iPhone's Settings for Weekly. Focus modes and notification settings can silence or delay alerts.

How do I get help with my account or data?

Email us using the address above for account access, privacy questions, or deletion requests. Describe what you need help with, without including your password.

Can I suggest a feature?

Absolutely. Join our community at r/WeeklyTheWeekPlanner to share feature ideas, report bugs, and help shape Weekly. You can also email us using the address above. Please keep personal information and account details out of public posts.
